How I work

From first idea to the final report

I handle all five steps myself, so nothing gets lost between a strategist, a designer and an editor.

  1. Plan

    Campaigns and calendar

    I plan campaigns and seasonal promotions with you, and set a posting schedule that is easy to keep.

  2. Create

    Photo, video, design

    I shoot the photos and videos, edit the reels and design the graphics.

  3. Publish

    Social, web, email

    I post across your channels and keep your website and email campaigns up to date.

  4. Partner

    KOLs and vloggers

    I reach out to creators and set up collaborations that put your brand in front of new people.

  5. Measure

    Simple reports

    I track reach, engagement and leads, and show you before and after numbers.
